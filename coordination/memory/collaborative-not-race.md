The user, 2026-11-22: "Not a fan of pre-proving. While I'd like to see lots of closed issues, this is a collaborative effort, not a race."

**Why:** the project is a shared effort with the integrator's agents and other contributors. Racing them for claims, or preparing proofs to grab issues the moment they appear, works against that, even if it raises our count.

**How to apply:**
- Never prove sorries that have no proof issue yet in order to claim them first.
- The sieve may still pre-compute hits (CPU only). When issues appear, hand in at a normal pace.
- Don't frame work as "beating" the sweepers. When a file is held, help through hints, not competing PRs. When someone else claimed first, step back (as with #2517).
- Still aim for many closed issues through genuinely unclaimed work.
